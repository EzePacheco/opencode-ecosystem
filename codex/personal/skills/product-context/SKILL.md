# Product context

Use this skill for whole-product knowledge coherence and Product authority
reconciliation. Select the smallest relevant workflow; invoking the skill does
not itself call for a context pack:

- **AUDIT / REFRESH** — assess authority, coverage, currency and material drift.
- **INGEST / RECONCILE** — classify supplied sources and plan or apply an
  authorized reconciliation.
- **PROJECT BRIDGE** — optionally prepare only the Product context a ChatGPT
  Project needs when a real access or context gap exists.

Resolve Product intent from designated Product authorities and decisions;
resolve implementation from current repository/runtime evidence. A ChatGPT
Space is Product authority only when explicitly designated, and only its
designated Pages with adequate provenance, status and scope qualify. Products
may use other authorities, and migration may be partial. See [authority
resolution](references/authority-resolution.md).

ChatGPT may use authorized Space/Pages or connected apps exposed by its active
surface. Codex Local does not thereby gain access to Space, Project, Sources,
Library or chat content. For engineering, use Product decisions explicitly
transferred by the orchestrator or canonical references actually accessible to
Codex. See [workflows](references/workflows.md) and [Space, Project and
engineering handoff](references/chatgpt-project.md).
